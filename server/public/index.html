Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. If you can answer a few questions about your business and pick photos from your camera roll, you can build a Frontage site.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on the Business plan. Buy a domain anywhere (we show you how), point it at Frontage in two steps, and we handle the rest, including the security certificate.

What happens to messages from my website?

Every contact form message lands in the Frontage app and in your email, with a notification on your phone, so you never miss a customer.

What if I don't like the first version?

Ask for changes in plain language, as many times as you like within your plan. You can also start over from your answers, and undo any change.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your Apple ID subscriptions. Your site stays live until the end of the period you paid for.
