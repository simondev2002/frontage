3. Service providers

We share data with providers that help us run Frontage, under contracts that restrict their use of it: Anthropic (AI generation of designs and text; receives your business details, photos and instructions), Apple (sign-in and payments), Stripe (payments made on our website, if offered), Resend (transactional email), and our hosting provider. Your published website is public by design; anything you place on it is visible to anyone.

AI processing needs your permission. Before your business details, photos or change requests are sent to Anthropic, the app asks you to agree, and it records when you did. Anthropic processes this data only to produce your website and does not use it to train its models. You can withdraw your permission at any time in Settings › Privacy; AI features then ask again before they run.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We process data to perform our contract with you (building and hosting your site, billing), for our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse, improving the Service), and with your consent where required (push notifications). Where providers are outside the EEA/UK, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

5. Your rights

You can access and correct your data in the app, and delete your account from Settings, which removes your sites, photos and messages. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Email us to exercise any right.

6. Visitors to Frontage websites

If you send a message through a website hosted by Frontage, the business owner receives it and is responsible for how it is used. We store it to deliver it and to fight spam. Hosted websites do not use tracking cookies; embedded maps are provided by Google and subject to Google's privacy policy.

7. Children

Frontage is for business owners and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We will post updates here and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
