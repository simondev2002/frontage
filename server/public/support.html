Support

Email hello@frontageweb.com and a human replies within one business day.

Billing

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To change or cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Custom domains

On the Business plan, open your site in the app, tap Publish, then Custom domain. We show the two DNS records to add at your domain provider. Changes can take up to an hour to be visible; tap "Check again" to verify.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your websites, photos and messages.

Report a website

Every hosted website has a "Report" link in its footer, or use this form.
